## 2. Two corrections found by a model check of 2026-09-29

A model double-check of the six GPT-5.6-Sol base proofs of the planted-defect study against Chen's notes (Claude Fable 5.1
agents with an adversarial verifier; a model check, not a human or expert review) also read the ledger's P1 and P6 entries. It
found two ledger issues. Neither concerns a base proof, and neither changes any recorded verdict.

- **P6, L6.2 [R] (LEDGER_v2.md only).** The parenthetical "equivalently, every ≺-minimal term has all its prime factors in P" is
  only a sufficient condition for the stated requirement ("for every x >= a_1, whether x is a term depends only on which primes
  of P divide x") when P is held fixed, not an equivalent. Example: for a_1 = 10 the terms are the even numbers from 10, so P =
  {2} governs membership, while the ≺-minimal terms 10, 12 and 14 have the prime factors 5, 3 and 7. Read as two existence
  statements (some finite P exists), the two forms are equivalent. A rater applying the parenthetical literally to a fixed P
  could wrongly mark L6.2 as not established. No recorded judgment used it: the parenthetical is new in v2, and every stage-1
  and stage-2 judgment used v1 (LEDGER_v2.md, version note).
- **P1, L1.1 (LEDGER.md and LEDGER_v2.md).** The explicit survivor argument ("at the end at most one integer > 1 (two would
  allow a move) and at least one (a move never outputs two 1s: if gcd(m, n) = 1 the second output is mn > 1)") is attributed to
  "Chen, p. 4", but Chen writes only "This already implies the result." The elaboration is the ledger's own gloss. It is correct;
  its parenthetical names only the gcd(m, n) = 1 case and leaves implicit that when the gcd g exceeds 1 the first output g is
  greater than 1. The rest of the P1 entry (the answer, the page, L1.1's "permanently increases the number of 1s", L1.2) matches
  Chen.
