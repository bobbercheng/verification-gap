# Addendum of 2026-09-29 to PROTOCOL.md, DEVIATIONS.md and recovery.json (camera-ready)

The files of this directory are kept exactly as they were released with the reviewed version of 2026-09-07; this dated file
corrects and completes them instead. No grader or study model was called for it, no experiment was run, and no stored request, response, judgment or count was
changed.

## 1. The two arms were not given the same information

PROTOCOL.md describes the ordinary-review baseline as "at the same budget", and the reviewed paper said that proposers and graders
"saw only the rubric and the text" and that "what is matched is the number of first-stage calls and the checking rule, not tokens
or time". Both arms did make the same number of first-stage calls (one per text and model) and their objections went through the
same checking rule, but "the rubric" named two different documents. Read from the stored `propose/*/*/request.json` and
`baseline/*/*/request.json`:

- The proposers' system prompt contains the problem's marking scheme (the closure rubric: "A complete proof needs ..."; the
  answer on P3, P4 and P5; example wrong approaches on P1, P4, P5 and P6), with its return block replaced by the audit
  instructions, which include "Do not repair the argument for the author". Length in characters, P1/P3/P4/P5/P6:
  5692/4072/6257/5519/4591.
- The graders' system prompt is the 0-7 coordinator scale, which places a small, straightforwardly repairable error at 5-6 and
  says "Do not consult, recall, or compare against any published solution, and do not use a fixed list of required lemmas".
  Length 2039/1957/2064/1575/1807.

The arms are therefore matched only in first-stage call count and checking rule, not in information (reference-guided against
reference-free), instruction, tokens or time. The same prompts are used by the witness and grade gates of the planted-defect
study (planted_defects/ADDENDUM_2026-09-29.md). The checkers could also read which procedure produced an objection (DEVIATIONS.md,
entry of 14:44 UTC).

## 2. Protocol status

PROTOCOL.md is prespecified and self-timestamped, not registered: its timestamps are self-authored, and the first commit that
contains it (2026-09-06 07:08:59 UTC) postdates the first calls (smoke test from 04:31 UTC). "Fixed before any call" in the
reviewed paper is corrected to this.

## 3. Who made the judgments in recovery.json

recovery.json calls its target-match and conclusion-change entries "post hoc author judgments", and the reviewed paper said they
were "judged by the author". They were produced by a model (Claude, in Claude Code) under the author's direction and reviewed by
the author, at the time and from the material its `_note` states (04:48 UTC, from the first-stage outputs, before any checker
verdict was read).

## 4. "Review" in these records

"The v6 acceptance review" (DEVIATIONS.md) and "the review" that found the unimplemented checker blinding are internal
pre-submission reviews run by language models (OpenAI Codex, desktop application, gpt-6-astra); no human reviewer took part, and
they are not reviews by the workshop's referees. The same holds for "the v5 review" named in PROTOCOL.md.
