## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle-parameter coordinates (active):** Introduced $x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL$, $y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC$, and $z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK$. Sine-law coordinates in the two midpoint triangles give explicit formulas for $K,L$; the remaining incidences give two scalar equations.
- **Tangent Cartesian algebra (active):** Normalized $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(u,v)$ and set $p=\tan x,q=\tan y,s=\tan z$. Derived rational coordinates for $K,L$, equations $F=0,G=0$ encoding the lines $BL$ and $CK$, and a rational expression $H$ equivalent to equality of the powers of $M,N$ with respect to $(AKL)$. Symbolic checking shows that after using $F=0$, $H$ factors as a rational multiple of $G$. Thus the desired conclusion follows algebraically; the remaining task is to simplify this factorization into a presentable hand-verifiable identity and rigorously control signs/possible zero denominators.
- **Power reformulation:** If the circle $(AKL)$ has equation $X^2+Y^2+DX+EY=0$, then $OM=ON$ is equivalent to equal powers of $M=(1/2,0)$ and $N=(u/2,v/2)$, namely
  \[u^2+v^2-1+2D(u-1)+2Ev=0.\]

## Current best

\[
K=\left(\frac{2p+s}{2(p+s)},\frac{sp}{2(p+s)}\right),\qquad

\]
The angle/incidence conditions $BL$ and $CK$ produce equations $F=0,G=0$. Direct symbolic elimination verifies that the equal-power expression for $M,N$ vanishes modulo these equations. This is substantial progress but not yet an IMO-quality proof because the raw factorization is unwieldy and all geometric range conditions have not yet been used to avoid tangent singularities.
