## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle-parameter coordinates:** Set $x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL$, $y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC$, $z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK$. Normalize $A=(0,0),B=(2,0)$, so $M=(1,0)$, and put $C=2N$.
- **Raw Cartesian and complex elimination:** Both independently verified the theorem but initially produced unwieldy factors.
- **Compact tangent/vector factorization (main approach):** For $p=\tan x,q=\tan y,s=\tan z$, and a scaled ray parameter $t$, derive
  \[
  K=\left(\frac{2p+s}{p+s},\frac{ps}{p+s}\right),\qquad
  L=(2-t(1-pq),t(p+q)).
  \]
  Sine law in $CNL$ gives, in complex notation,
  \[
  L=\lambda N,\qquad \lambda=\frac{(2p+q)-ipq}{p+q}.
  \]
  Rotation through $x+z$ is represented up to scalar by
  \[
  T_{p,s}(u,v)=((1-ps)u-(p+s)v,(p+s)u+(1-ps)v).
  \]
  Hence the $CK$ incidence is $Q=(K-2N)\times T_{p,s}N=0$.
- **Power determinant:** Define
  \[
  D=\det\begin{pmatrix}K_x&K_y&|K|^2\\L_x&L_y&|L|^2\\N_x-1&N_y&|N|^2-1\end{pmatrix}.
  \]
  This determinant vanishes exactly when $M,N$ have equal powers to the circle through $0,K,L$.
- **Abstract vector simplification attempt:** For arbitrary complex multiplier $L=(U+iV)N$ and arbitrary rotation map, expressions for $D,Q$ were obtained in dot/cross invariants. Without substituting the special $U,V$ they remain complicated, so the special angle relation is essential.

## Current best
The exact compact identity
\[
\boxed{D=\frac{p(pq^2t+pt+q)}{(p+q)(p+s)}Q}
\]
has been checked symbolically. This immediately proves $D=0$ from the line $CK$. A standalone lemma recording the identity is in `lemmas/analytic-factorization.md`, and a second script is being prepared to verify the cross-multiplied identity directly from the displayed coordinates. The main open rigor issues are (i) supplying an organized hand expansion/proof of the boxed identity rather than citing computation, and (ii) avoiding undefined tangents/right-angle exceptional cases. A promising remedy for (ii) is to use homogeneous pairs $(\cos x,\sin x)$ rather than tangents and homogenize the boxed identity; alternatively one can rotate/reflection-normalize or split finitely many right-angle cases. Once these are handled, equal powers imply $OM=ON$ immediately.

## Full proof
Not final. In the generic finite-tangent case, all substantive geometric reasoning and the final algebraic implication are complete. The desired conclusion follows via
\[
\operatorname{Pow}_{(AKL)}(M)=\operatorname{Pow}_{(AKL)}(N)
\Longrightarrow OM^2-R^2=ON^2-R^2
\Longrightarrow OM=ON.
\]
