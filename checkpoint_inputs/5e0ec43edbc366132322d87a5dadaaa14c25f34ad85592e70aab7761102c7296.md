## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle coordinates:** Let $x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL$, $y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC$, $z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK$. Normalize $A=0,B=2$, hence $M=1$, and write $C=2N$ in the complex plane.
- **Raw Cartesian and complex elimination:** Both verified the theorem but initially gave long expressions.
- **Tangent/homogeneous vector factorization:** This produced a compact exact relation between the desired power determinant and the last line-incidence equation, with no right-angle exceptions.
- **Search for a conceptual proof of the factorization:** Abstracting the complex multipliers made expressions longer; the special sine-law relations are essential. A direct complex algebra lemma remains the cleanest route.

## Current best
The setup is
\[
K=1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+z)}e^{iz},\qquad
L=2-te^{-i(x+y)},\qquad
L=\lambda N,
\quad \lambda=1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+y)}e^{-iy},
\]
with $t=BL>0$. Writing $[u,v]=\operatorname{Im}(\bar uv)$, define
\[
Q=[K-2N,e^{i(x+z)}N]
\]
and
\[
D=[K,L](|N|^2-1)-[K,N-1]|L|^2+[L,N-1]|K|^2.
\]
The exact identity
\[
\boxed{D=\frac{\sin x(t\sin x+\sin y)}{\sin(x+y)\sin(x+z)}Q}
\]
has been independently verified in tangent, unit-exponential, and homogeneous trigonometric forms. The last angle condition gives $Q=0$, so $D=0$. The determinant $D$ is precisely the equality-of-powers condition for $M=1,N$ relative to $(0KL)$, yielding $OM=ON$.

A complex proof of the boxed identity can be made fully explicit by putting $X=e^{ix},Y=e^{iy},Z=e^{iz}$. One gets
\[
K=\frac{2X^2Z^2-Z^2-1}{X^2Z^2-1},\quad
\lambda=\frac{X^2Y^2+X^2-2}{X^2Y^2-1},\quad
L=2-\frac t{XY},\quad N=L/\lambda,
\]
and conjugation replaces $X,Y,Z$ by their inverses. Substituting these into the definitions and using $[u,v]=(\bar uv-u\bar v)/(2i)$ proves the identity. The current gap is solely expository: provide a concise but adequately checkable line-by-line algebra verification rather than “after simplification.”
