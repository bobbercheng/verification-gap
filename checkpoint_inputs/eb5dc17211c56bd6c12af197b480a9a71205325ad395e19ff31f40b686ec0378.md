## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

- **Normalized-length quadratics:** with $R=2BK/AB$ and $D=2CL/AC$, both angle constraints were converted into explicit quadratics, eliminating $y,z$.
- **Full symbolic elimination:** a Gröbner computation and numerical tests certify the analytic pipeline.
- **Compact quadratic $F$:** the constraints become $2Ub=cF(R)$ and $2Uc=bF(D)$ for $F(T)=V(T^2-2XT+2)-ST$.

- **Sparse certificate (major advance):** sequential polynomial division has produced a compact explicit identity expressing the target numerator as a combination of the two geometric constraints and only the unit-circle identities $C^2+S^2=1$, $X^2+U^2=1$. ## Full proof
A complete proof is nearly finished. The established geometric/trigonometric part is as follows.

Orient the triangle so that the interior rays have orders $BA,BK,BL,BC$ at $B$ and $CA,CL,CK,CB$ at $C$. Define
\[
\alpha=\angle BAC,\quad\beta=\angle ABC,\quad\gamma=\angle ACB,
\]
\[
x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL,\quad y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC,
\quad z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK.
\]
Sine law in $BMK,BCK$ gives
\[
BK=\frac c2\frac{\sin z}{\sin(x+z)}
=a\frac{\sin(\gamma-x-z)}{\sin(\alpha+2x+z)}. \tag{1}
\]
Likewise, sine law in $CNL,BCL$ gives
\[
CL=\frac b2\frac{\sin y}{\sin(x+y)}
=a\frac{\sin(\beta-x-y)}{\sin(\alpha+2x+y)}. \tag{2}
\]
For a generic pair $(\theta,t)=(\gamma,z)$ or $(\beta,y)$ and $\rho=\sin t/\sin(x+t)$, cancellation using the sine law of $ABC$ gives
\[
\sin\theta\sin t\sin(\alpha+2x+t)
=2\sin\alpha\sin(x+t)\sin(\theta-x-t).
\]
Dividing by $\sin^2t$ and using $\cot t=(1/\rho-\cos x)/\sin x$ yields
\[
-\rho^2\sin\theta\sin x\sin(\alpha+x)
+\rho\sin\theta(\cos(\alpha-x)-\cos x\cos(\alpha+2x))
-2\sin\alpha\sin x\sin(\theta-x)=0. \tag{3}
\]
Elementary trigonometric simplification and
\[
\cot\gamma=\frac{b-Cc}{Sc},\qquad \cot\beta=\frac{c-Cb}{Sb}
\]
turn (3) into $E_R=E_D=0$.

Coordinates (II) follow directly from ray directions. If the circle through $A,K,L$ is $\xi^2+\eta^2-P\xi-Q\eta=0$, Cramer's rule gives $P=P_0/\Delta,Q=Q_0/\Delta$. Its centre is $(P/2,Q/2)$ and
\[
OM^2-ON^2=-\frac{cP}{2}+\frac{b(CP+SQ)}{2}+\frac{c^2-b^2}{4}.
\]
Thus the desired result follows from
\[
b(CP+SQ)-cP=\frac{b^2-c^2}{2},
\]
which is equivalent to $\mathcal T=0$. Certificate (IV), after final sign/scaling verification, proves this immediately.
