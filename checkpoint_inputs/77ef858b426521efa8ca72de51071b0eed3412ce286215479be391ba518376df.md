## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- Numeric verification with scipy optimization over free angles α,β,γ: there exists a one-parameter family of pairs (K,L) satisfying the conditions; for every tested solution OM = ON holds to ~1e-10. The claim is true.
- Special case AB = AC: the configuration is symmetric under reflection in the angle bisector of ∠BAC precisely when β = γ. That bisector is also the perpendicular bisector of MN, so O lies on it and OM = ON. Done for isosceles.
- Angle notation: set α = ∠KBA = ∠ACL, β = ∠LBK = ∠LNC, γ = ∠LCK = ∠BMK.
  Then triangle BMK has angles α at B, γ at M, 180°-α-γ at K.
  Triangle CNL has angles α at C, β at N, 180°-α-β at L.

  BK = (AB/2)·sin γ / sin(α+γ), MK = (AB/2)·sin α / sin(α+γ),
