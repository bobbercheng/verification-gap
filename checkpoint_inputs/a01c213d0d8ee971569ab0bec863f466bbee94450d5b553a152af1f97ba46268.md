## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Power-of-a-point reformulation (successful).** $OM=ON \iff \mathrm{pow}(B,\omega)-\mathrm{pow}(C,\omega)=(AB^2-AC^2)/2$ where $\omega=(AKL)$, via the median-length formula.
- **Auxiliary points $X,Y$ (successful).** $Y:=$ ray $BK\cap AC$, $X:=$ ray $CL\cap AB$. The three angle conditions translate (directed angles) into three concyclicities: $(BXYC)=\omega_1$, $(CMKX)=\omega_K$, $(BNLY)=\omega_L$ (tangency of $AB$ to $\omega_K$ at $M$ when $X=M$; similarly for $Y=N$).
- **Affine-linear power identity (the key step).** $F(Z):=\mathrm{pow}(Z,\omega)-\mathrm{pow}(Z,\omega_K)-\mathrm{pow}(Z,\omega_L)+\mathrm{pow}(Z,\omega_1)$ is affine-linear and vanishes at $A$, $K$, $L$ (non-collinear) hence $F\equiv 0$. Evaluating at $B$, $C$ gives $\mathrm{pow}(B,\omega)=BM\cdot BX$, $\mathrm{pow}(C,\omega)=CN\cdot CY$, and $\mathrm{pow}(A,\omega_1)$ gives $AB\cdot AX = AC\cdot AY$; the median formula finishes.
- Numerically verified end-to-end in `code/verify.py` (several triangles, generic and $X=M$ tangency case): original hypotheses hold in constructed configs, $OM=ON$, $F\equiv 0$, all power formulas exact to machine precision.
