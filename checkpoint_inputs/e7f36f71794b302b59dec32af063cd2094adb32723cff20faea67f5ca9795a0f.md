## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle coordinates:** Set $x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL$, $y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC$, $z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK$. Normalize $A=(0,0),B=(2,0)$, so $M=(1,0)$, and put $C=2N$.
- **Raw Cartesian/complex elimination:** Verified the theorem but produced unwieldy factors.
- **Tangent-vector factorization:** Found the target determinant as a scalar multiple of the incidence determinant.
- **Homogeneous trigonometric factorization (main):** Sine law and ray directions give
  \[
  K=1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+z)}e^{iz},\quad
  L=2-te^{-i(x+y)},\quad
  L=\lambda N,\quad
  \lambda=1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+y)}e^{-iy},
  \]
  where $t=BL>0$. The $CK$ condition is
  \[
  Q=[K-2N,e^{i(x+z)}N]=0,
  \]
  where $[u,v]=\operatorname{Im}(\bar u v)$.
- **Power determinant:** Set
  \[
  D=[K,L](|N|^2-1)-[K,N-1]|L|^2+[L,N-1]|K|^2.
  \]
  Then $D=0$ is exactly equality of powers of $M=1$ and $N$ to the circle through $0,K,L$.

## Current best
The key exact identity is
\[
\boxed{D=\frac{\sin x(t\sin x+\sin y)}{\sin(x+y)\sin(x+z)}Q.}
\]
It has now been verified in three independent exact forms: tangent Cartesian, unit-exponential complex, and homogeneous sine/cosine algebra. The homogeneous version uses no division by individual cosines, so all right-angle cases are covered. A lemma file records a complex-substitution proof outline. Attempts to generate a short coefficient table showed that a brute-force six-variable sine/cosine expansion is still long; the most readable rigorous presentation will likely state the boxed elementary algebra identity and verify it by a compact complex substitution $X=e^{ix},Y=e^{iy},Z=e^{iz}$, noting conjugation sends these to their reciprocals. The remaining concern is expository rather than mathematical: include enough intermediate algebra that an IMO grader accepts the factorization rather than treating it as an unexplained calculation.

Angle ranges are under control: from triangles $BMK$ and $CNL$, $x,z>0$, $x+z<\pi$ and $x,y>0$, $x+y<\pi$, so all sine denominators in the formulas are positive. From the hypothesis that $CK$ has direction opposite to $R_{x+z}N$, the cross product $Q$ vanishes. Thus the boxed identity gives $D=0$, and equal powers yield $OM=ON$.
