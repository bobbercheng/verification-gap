## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle-parameter coordinates:** Set
  \[x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL,\quad y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC,\quad z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK.
  \]
  Normalize $A=(0,0),B=(2,0)$, hence $M=(1,0)$, and write $C=2N$. Sine law gives simple coordinates for $K$ and the complex multiplier taking $N$ to $L$.
- **Raw tangent Cartesian algebra:** An earlier elimination proved the desired relation but was very large.
- **Complex unit-exponential factorization:** Also proved that the equal-power expression is a multiple of the $CK$ incidence equation, but the coefficient was unwieldy.
- **Improved tangent/vector factorization (successful and compact):** Put $p=\tan x,q=\tan y,s=\tan z$. Parametrize the ray $BL$ by a positive scaled parameter $t$:
  \[
  K=\left(\frac{2p+s}{p+s},\frac{ps}{p+s}\right),\qquad
  L=(2-t(1-pq),t(p+q)).
  \]
  Identifying vectors with complex numbers, sine law in $CNL$ says
  \[
  L=\lambda N,\qquad \lambda=\frac{(2p+q)-ipq}{p+q}.
  \]
  The $CK$ direction is represented, up to a positive scalar, by
  \[
  T_{p,s}(u,v)=((1-ps)u-(p+s)v,(p+s)u+(1-ps)v).
  \]
  Thus its incidence equation is
  \[
  Q:=(K-2N)\times T_{p,s}N=0.
  \]
  Let
  \[
  D=\det\begin{pmatrix}
  K_x&K_y&|K|^2\\
  L_x&L_y&|L|^2\\
  N_x-1&N_y&|N|^2-1
  \end{pmatrix}.
  \]
  Exact simplification yields the striking compact identity
  \[
  \boxed{D=\frac{p(pq^2t+pt+q)}{(p+q)(p+s)}Q.}
  \]
  Therefore $Q=0$ gives $D=0$, precisely equality of the powers of $M,N$ to $(AKL)$.

## Current best
The full proof is essentially complete, modulo converting the boxed identity from a computer-discovered formula into a short, transparently hand-checkable lemma and handling possible right-angle cases where one of $\tan x,\tan y,\tan z$ is undefined. Since $x,y,z>0$, $x+y<\pi$ and $x+z<\pi$, but individual sums/angles might cross $\pi/2$. A safer final version may use cotangents or continuity; alternatively, prove first for finite nonzero tangents with all relevant denominators nonzero and invoke perturbation only if existence varies continuously (which itself needs care). The identity itself is recorded in `lemmas/analytic-factorization.md`; a manual derivation is being sought using abstract multiplier components.

## Full proof
Not final yet. The intended last steps are: prove the determinant identity; infer $D=0$ from the line $CK$; interpret $D=0$ as equal powers; then

OM^2-R^2=\operatorname{Pow}_{(AKL)}(M)=\operatorname{Pow}_{(AKL)}(N)=ON^2-R^2,
\]
