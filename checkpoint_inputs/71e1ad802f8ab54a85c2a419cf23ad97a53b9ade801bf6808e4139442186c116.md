## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle-parameter coordinates (active):** Put
  \[x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL,\quad y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC,\quad z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK.
  \]
  Normalize $A=0,B=2,C=2a$ in the complex plane, so $M=1,N=a$. Sine law in $BMK$ and $CNL$ gives
  \[k=1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+z)}e^{iz},\qquad l=a\left(1+\frac{\sin x}{\sin(x+y)}e^{-iy}\right).
  \]
  Since the ray $BL$ makes angle $\pi-x-y$ with the positive real axis, write
  \[l=2-te^{-i(x+y)},\qquad t=BL>0.
  \]
- **Tangent Cartesian algebra:** Rational coordinates and the two remaining incidence equations were derived. Symbolic elimination verifies the result, but the expansion is unsuitable for presentation.
- **Complex factorization (successful algebraically):** With $X=e^{ix},Y=e^{iy},Z=e^{iz}$, substitute the three formulas above into the circumcenter formula
  \[o=\frac{|k|^2l-|l|^2k}{\bar k l-\bar l k}.
  \]
  The condition that $CK$ has the prescribed direction is
  \[r:=\frac{k-2a}{aXZ}\in\mathbb R.
  \]
  Exact symbolic factorization has now established the identity
  \[
  o(\bar a-1)+\bar o(a-1)+1-|a|^2
  =R(X,Y,Z,t)\,(r-\bar r),
  \]
  where $R$ is an explicit rational factor. Therefore the result is proved in principle. The current task is to replace the unwieldy exponential form of $R$ by a compact determinant/trigonometric derivation, or to present a short standalone algebra lemma whose verification is manageable by hand.
- **Determinant reformulation (active):** Equality $OM=ON$ can be expressed without explicitly solving for $O$. For any circle through $0,k,l$, the points $1$ and $a$ have equal powers exactly when the determinant
  \[
  \det\begin{pmatrix}
  \Re k&\Im k&|k|^2\\
  \Re l&\Im l&|l|^2\\
  \Re(a-1)&\Im(a-1)&|a|^2-1
  \end{pmatrix}=0.
  \]
  The aim is to factor this determinant directly by the cross-product equation for the line $CK$.

## Current best
A fully exact computer-assisted identity proves the key implication, and the geometric setup/orientations have been numerically validated. More concretely, after all conditions except the direction of $CK$ are built into $k,l,a$, the target equal-power expression is an explicit nonzero rational multiple of $r-\bar r$, where $r=(k-2a)/(ae^{i(x+z)})$. The last angle condition makes $r$ real (indeed negative, since $L$ lies inside $\angle ACK$), so the target vanishes. The proof still needs a human-readable factorization and complete angle-range/nondegeneracy treatment.
