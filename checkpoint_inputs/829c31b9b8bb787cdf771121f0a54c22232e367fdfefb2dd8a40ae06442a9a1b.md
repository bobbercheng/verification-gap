## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Angle parameters and polar coordinates.** Put $p=\angle KBA=\angle ACL$, $q=\angle LBK=\angle LNC$, $r=\angle LCK=\angle BMK$, $x=\angle BAK$, $y=\angle CAL$, and $\delta=\angle KAL$. The midpoint conditions and sine rule reduce the problem to a trigonometric factorization.
- **Circle intersections with $AB,AC$.** If the circumcircle of $AKL$ meets the oriented lines $AB,AC$ again at $D,E$, with $AD=d,AE=e$, then $OM=ON$ is exactly $AB\cdot d-AC\cdot e=(AB^2-AC^2)/2$.
- **Numerical checking.** `code/explore_trig.py` solves the compatibility equation and checks the target. `code/symbolic_poly.py` exposed the exact harmonic factorization.
- **Harmonic factorization (essentially complete).** The exact proportionality constant has now been identified: the target residual equals $\sin(x-y)$ times the compatibility residual. The variable Fourier coefficients have a short proof. Constant coefficients reduce to one auxiliary identity, equation (8) below; numerical and expansion checks confirm it, but its clean derivation is the sole remaining algebraic gap.

## Current best
The exact missing identity is
\[
T=\sin(x-y)\bigl(PQ\sin U\sin V-\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q)\bigr).
\]
Its $e^{\pm2i\delta}$ coefficients agree immediately from $\sin p/P=\sin(p+x)$ and $\sin p/Q=\sin(p+y)$. Equality of constant terms is equivalent to
\[
[2(P\sin y-Q\sin x)+\sin(x-y)]\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q)=\sin^2p\sin(q+x-r-y).\tag{8}
\]
The sole open task is to present a concise derivation of (8) from $\cot x=\cot p+2\cot r$ and $\cot y=\cot p+2\cot q$. Everything else is complete.

## Full proof
Let
\[
p=\angle KBA=\angle ACL,\quad q=\angle LBK=\angle LNC,\quad r=\angle LCK=\angle BMK,
\]
and put $x=\angle BAK$, $y=\angle CAL$, and $\delta=\angle KAL$. The interior assumptions imply that the rays occur in the stated orders, so that $\angle BAC=x+\delta+y$.

Put $c=AB$, $b=AC$. Applying the sine rule first in $BMK$ and $ABK$, and using $BM=c/2$, gives
\[
\frac{c\sin x}{\sin(p+x)}=BK=\frac{c\sin r}{2\sin(p+r)}.
\]
Hence
\[
\cot x=\cot p+2\cot r,\qquad AK=cP,\quad P:=\frac{\sin p}{\sin(p+x)}. \tag{A}
\]
Similarly,
\[
\cot y=\cot p+2\cot q,\qquad AL=bQ,\quad Q:=\frac{\sin p}{\sin(p+y)}. \tag{B}
\]
The sine rule in $ACK$ and $ABL$ now gives, with $U=p+r+\delta+y$ and $V=p+q+\delta+x$,
\[
\frac cbP=\frac{\sin(p+r)}{\sin U},\qquad
\frac bcQ=\frac{\sin(p+q)}{\sin V}. \tag{1}
\]
In particular,
\[
PQ\sin U\sin V=\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q).\tag{C}
\]

Let the circle through $A,K,L$ meet the oriented lines $AB,AC$ again at $D,E$, and set $AD=d$, $AE=e$ as directed lengths. If $\mathbf w=2\overrightarrow{AO}$, the equation of this circle (with $A$ as origin) is $|\mathbf z|^2=\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf z$; hence $d$ and $e$ are the projections of $\mathbf w$ on the rays $AB$ and $AC$. Since $M,N$ have position vectors $(c/2)\mathbf e_B,(b/2)\mathbf e_C$, direct expansion shows that $OM=ON$ is equivalent to
\[
cd-be=\frac{c^2-b^2}{2}. \tag{2}
\]
Writing the circle equation at $K,L$ and solving the resulting two linear equations gives
\[
d=\frac{cP\sin(\delta+x)-bQ\sin x}{\sin\delta},\qquad
e=\frac{-cP\sin y+bQ\sin(\delta+y)}{\sin\delta}. \tag{3}
\]
The relation $\cot x=\cot p+2\cot r$ implies, by expansion,
\[
P\sin(\delta+x)-\frac12\sin\delta
=\frac{\sin p}{2\sin(p+r)}\sin(\delta+r), \tag{4}
\]
and analogously
\[
Q\sin(\delta+y)-\frac12\sin\delta
=\frac{\sin p}{2\sin(p+q)}\sin(\delta+q). \tag{5}
\]
Substitution of (3)--(5) shows that (2) is equivalent to
\[
\frac cb\frac{\sin p\sin(\delta+r)}{2\sin(p+r)}-
\frac bc\frac{\sin p\sin(\delta+q)}{2\sin(p+q)}+P\sin y-Q\sin x=0. \tag{6}
\]
Using (1), this is equivalent to $T=0$, where
\[
\begin{aligned}
T={}&Q\sin p\sin(\delta+r)\sin V-P\sin p\sin(\delta+q)\sin U\\
&+2(P\sin y-Q\sin x)\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q). \tag{7}
\end{aligned}
\]
We claim the exact factorization
\[
T=\sin(x-y)[PQ\sin U\sin V-\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q)]. \tag{F}
\]
The proof is recorded in `lemmas/trigonometric-factorization.md`: product-to-sum comparison proves all variable harmonics immediately, and the constant term reduces to
\[
[2(P\sin y-Q\sin x)+\sin(x-y)]\sin(p+r)\sin(p+q)
=\sin^2p\sin(q+x-r-y). \tag{8}
\]
A clean hand derivation of (8) remains to be inserted. Once (F) is complete, (C) makes its right side zero, proving (7), then (6), (2), and $OM=ON$.
