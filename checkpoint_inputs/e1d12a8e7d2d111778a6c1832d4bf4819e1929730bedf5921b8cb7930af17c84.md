## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- **Power/circle-equation reformulation:** $OM=ON$ is equivalent to equal powers of $M,N$ to $(AKL)$; in coordinates it is a linear relation between circle coefficients.
- **Angle parametrization and sine law:** exact sine-law formulas for $BK,CL$ were obtained using $\alpha,\beta,\gamma,x,y,z$.
- **Normalized-length quadratics:** with $R=2BK/AB$ and $D=2CL/AC$, both angle constraints were converted into explicit quadratics, eliminating $y,z$.
- **Full symbolic elimination:** a Gröbner computation certifies the target from the derived identities; numerical tests verify the pipeline.
- **Compact quadratic $F$:** the constraints become $2Ub=cF(R)$ and $2Uc=bF(D)$ for $F(T)=V(T^2-2XT+2)-ST$.
- **Cartesian/complex-coordinate rewrite:** ordinary coordinates yield simple point and squared-length formulas and a 31-term polynomial numerator for the target.
- **Sequential division by constraints (new):** dividing the target polynomial first by $E_R=2Ub-cF(R)$ as a polynomial in $R$, and then by $E_D=2Uc-bF(D)$ as a polynomial in $D$, gives unexpectedly compact quotients. Both quotients have the common denominator $4V$ and are displayed below. The residual should be a combination of the three elementary identities; extracting that combination is now much easier than attacking the original target.
- **Synthetic/power approach:** logged separately; no concrete construction yet.

## Current best
Normalize the circumdiameter of $ABC$ to $1$, and set
\[
S=\sin\alpha,\ C=\cos\alpha,\ U=\sin x,\ X=\cos x,\ V=SX+CU,
\quad R=\frac{2BK}{c},\ D=\frac{2CL}{b},
\]
\[
F(T)=V(T^2-2XT+2)-ST,
\quad E_R=2Ub-cF(R),\quad E_D=2Uc-bF(D). \tag{I}
\]
The geometry gives $E_R=E_D=0$. In Cartesian coordinates,
\[
K=(c-cRX/2,cRU/2),\quad
L=(bC-bD(CX-SU)/2,bS-bDV/2). \tag{II}
\]
Let $\Delta=K_xL_y-K_yL_x$, and define the numerators
\[
P_0=AK^2L_y-K_yAL^2,\qquad Q_0=K_xAL^2-AK^2L_x.
\]
Then the circle coefficients are $P=P_0/\Delta,Q=Q_0/\Delta$. After clearing denominators, define
\[
T=2\{b(CP_0+SQ_0)-cP_0\}-(b^2-c^2)\Delta. \tag{III}
\]
The desired result is $T=0$. Exact polynomial division gives
\[
T=A E_R+B E_D+H, \tag{IV}
\]
where
\[
A=\frac{bc}{4V}\bigl(C^2DUb-CDUc+DS^2Ub-DSXc+2Sc\bigr), \tag{V}
\]
\[
B=\frac{bc}{4V}\bigl(CRUb+RSXb-RUc-2Sb\bigr), \tag{VI}
\]
and $H$ is a 34-term residual saved in `scratch/reduceconstraints.txt`. Since $V=\sin(\alpha+x)>0$, the division is legitimate. A direct Gröbner reduction already guarantees
\[
H\in (S^2+C^2-1,\ U^2+X^2-1,\ b^2+c^2-2Cbc-S^2).
\]
The immediate task is to clear $4V$ and sequentially divide $H$ by those three simple identities to obtain explicit, preferably factored, multipliers. This should complete a rigorous algebraic lemma.

## Full proof
A complete human-readable proof has not yet been obtained. The established portion is as follows.

Orient the triangle so that the interior rays have orders $BA,BK,BL,BC$ at $B$ and $CA,CL,CK,CB$ at $C$. Define
\[
\alpha=\angle BAC,\quad\beta=\angle ABC,\quad\gamma=\angle ACB,
\]
\[
x=\angle KBA=\angle ACL,\quad y=\angle LBK=\angle LNC,
\quad z=\angle LCK=\angle BMK.
\]
Sine law in $BMK,BCK$ gives
\[
BK=\frac c2\frac{\sin z}{\sin(x+z)}
=a\frac{\sin(\gamma-x-z)}{\sin(\alpha+2x+z)}. \tag{1}
\]
Likewise, sine law in $CNL,BCL$ gives
\[
CL=\frac b2\frac{\sin y}{\sin(x+y)}
=a\frac{\sin(\beta-x-y)}{\sin(\alpha+2x+y)}. \tag{2}
\]
For a generic pair $(\theta,t)=(\gamma,z)$ or $(\beta,y)$ and $\rho=\sin t/\sin(x+t)$, cancellation using the sine law of $ABC$ gives
\[
\sin\theta\sin t\sin(\alpha+2x+t)
=2\sin\alpha\sin(x+t)\sin(\theta-x-t).
\]
Dividing by $\sin^2t$ and using $\cot t=(1/\rho-\cos x)/\sin x$ yields
\[
-\rho^2\sin\theta\sin x\sin(\alpha+x)
+\rho\sin\theta(\cos(\alpha-x)-\cos x\cos(\alpha+2x))
-2\sin\alpha\sin x\sin(\theta-x)=0. \tag{3}
\]
Elementary trigonometric simplification and
\[
\cot\gamma=\frac{b-Cc}{Sc},\qquad \cot\beta=\frac{c-Cb}{Sb}
\]
turn (3) into $E_R=E_D=0$.

Coordinates (II) follow directly from the ray directions. If the circle through $A,K,L$ is $X_0^2+Y_0^2-PX_0-QY_0=0$, then Cramer's rule gives $P=P_0/\Delta,Q=Q_0/\Delta$. Its centre is $(P/2,Q/2)$ and
\[
OM^2-ON^2=-\frac{cP}{2}+\frac{b(CP+SQ)}{2}+\frac{c^2-b^2}{4}.
\]
Thus $T=0$ proves the result. Identity (IV) reduces this to the elementary residual factorization still to be written explicitly.
