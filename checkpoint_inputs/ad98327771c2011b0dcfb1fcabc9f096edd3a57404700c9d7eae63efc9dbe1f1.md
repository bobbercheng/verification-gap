## Problem
Let $n$ be a positive integer. Liu Bang and Xiang Yu have a stick of length $1$ and want to divide it between themselves. Liu marks at most $n$ points on the stick, and then Xiang marks at most $n$ points on the stick. The marked points are distinct. Then, the stick is cut at all marked points, creating a number of pieces. Afterwards, they take turns claiming any unclaimed piece of the stick, with Liu going first. Each player's goal is to maximise the total length of their own pieces. For each $n$, determine the largest value $c$ such that Liu may guarantee a total length of at least $c$, regardless of Xiang's play.

## Approaches tried
- **Draft-game reduction** (verified `code/draft.py` on 3000 random multisets): with optimal alternating claiming, Liu gets the odd order-statistics sum $b_1+b_3+\dots$ of sorted piece lengths. So $c(n)=\max_{\text{Liu cuts}}\min_{\text{Xiang cuts}}$ odd-sum. (Greedy-optimality lemma still needs writing up.)
- **Grid max-min** (`code/game.py`): $n=1$: $2/3$; $n=2$: $4/7$; $n=3$: $8/15$ ⇒ **CONJECTURE $c(n)=\frac{2^n}{2^{n+1}-1}$**, Liu playing dyadic pieces $(1,2,4,\dots,2^n)/(2^{n+1}-1)$.
- **Key reformulation**: odd-sum $=\frac12(1+\mathrm{alt})$, alt $=b_1-b_2+b_3-\dots=|\{t:N(t)\text{ odd}\}|=|\triangle_i[0,b_i)|$. Both bounds ⇔ alt vs $1/M$, $M=2^{n+1}-1$. Also: removing equal pairs never changes alt; cutting piece $\ell$ into $x,\ell-x$ toggles $S\triangle\{\ell,x,\ell-x\}$ in the reduced set model, and toggles $[0,\delta)\cup[\ell-\delta,\ell)$ in $O$, $\delta=\min(x,\ell-x)$.
- **LB toggle relaxation** (`code/bfs.py`,`fine.py`,`table.py`,`seq.py`): $g(n,k)$ = min $|O|$ after $\le k$ toggles $[0,\delta)\cup[p,p+\delta)$ ($\delta\le p$, $p+\delta\le2^n$) from $O_0^{(n)}=\triangle_{i=0}^n[0,2^i)$. BFS (scales 1,2,4,8) ⇒ conjecture $g(n,k)=g(n-1,k-1)$ for $k\ge1$, $g(n,0)=(2^{n+1}+(-1)^n)/3$, hence $g(n,n)=1$ (= needed bound $1/M$ in original units) and $g(n,n+1)=0$. **Proved: $g(n,k)\le g(n-1,k-1)$** (full toggle $[0,2^n)$ sends $n$-instance to $(n-1)$-instance, then play smaller-instance moves). **OPEN (crux): $g(n,k)\ge g(n-1,k-1)$.** Fold at $2^{n-1}$ maps to $(n-1)$-instance but straddling toggles become illegal "free pairs"; kill sequence for $k=n+1$ is toggles $[0,2^i)\cup[2^i,2^{i+1})$. See scratch/lb-notes.md.
- **UB set game** (`code/setgame2.py`): Xiang restricted to reduced sets (cancel equal pairs; move = toggle $\{\ell,x,\ell-x\}$, $\ell\in S$) is a valid restriction (set-game value ≥ true Xiang optimum). DP on fine grids (B=2,4; n=2,3) ⇒ value $\le T/M_n$ for ALL starts with $\ge2$ pieces; only violations are single tiny pieces (e.g. $S=\{1\}$, $T\le4$), pure grid-granularity artifacts (unit piece can't be cut finely on coarse grid). So UB conjecturally holds via set game; explicit general strategy still to be extracted (n=1: halve if $a_1\ge2T/3$ else clone; n=2: 4-branch strategy worked out by hand; general $n$: 1-move schemes (halve largest / clone 2nd) + 2-move combo $\{a,b,c\}\to\{|a-b-c|\}$ giving reduction $2\min(a,b+c)$ + recursion; need covering argument over all cases).
