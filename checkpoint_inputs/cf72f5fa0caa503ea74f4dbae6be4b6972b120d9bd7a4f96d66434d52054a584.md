## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- Numeric verification (scipy): 1-parameter family; OM=ON always (to 1e-10). Claim true.
- Angles: α=∠KBA=∠ACL, β=∠LBK=∠LNC, γ=∠LCK=∠BMK.
  △BMK ~ angles (α at B, γ at M, 180-α-γ at K).
  △CNL ~ angles (α at C, β at N, 180-α-β at L).
- Isosceles AB=AC: holds by reflection symmetry (β=γ, O on bisector = midperp of MN).
- Algebraic (A=0): need O·(C-B)=(|C|²-|B|²)/4 where O solves O·K=|K|²/2, O·L=|L|²/2.
- Tried (and stalled): bulk complex rotations in sympy; pure angle chase; empirical closed-form for αβγ relation (none simple).
