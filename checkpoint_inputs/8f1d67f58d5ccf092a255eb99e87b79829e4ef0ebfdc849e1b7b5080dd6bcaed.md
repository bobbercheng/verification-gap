## Problem
Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let points $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let points $K$ and $L$ be chosen inside triangles $BMC$ and $BNC$, respectively, such that $K$ lies inside the angle $LBA$, $L$ lies inside the angle $ACK$, and $\angle KBA = \angle ACL$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC$, $\angle LCK = \angle BMK$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $AKL$. Prove that $OM = ON$.

## Approaches tried
- Coordinate numerics with scipy optimization over α,β,γ: confirmed existence of a 1-parameter family of (K,L) satisfying the angle conditions, and for all of them OM=ON to machine precision (~1e-10). Also observed O always lies on the perp-bisector of MN (expected since that is OM=ON locus).
- Angle unpacking: set ∠KBA=∠ACL=α, ∠LBK=∠LNC=β, ∠LCK=∠BMK=γ. Then △BMK has angles α at B, γ at M, 180-α-γ at K. △CNL has angles α at C, β at N, 180-α-β at L. Construction via rays from B,M for K and C,N for L, then two conditions that BL and CK realize the remaining angles β,γ.
- Next: look for spiral similarities, isogonal lines, trigonometric Ceva, or complex numbers / vectors; or properties of circumcenter O (perp bisectors of AK,AL).
